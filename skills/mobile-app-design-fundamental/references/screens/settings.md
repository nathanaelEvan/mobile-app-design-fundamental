# Settings

**Job:** change preferences and account details quickly, then leave.

**Anatomy**
1. Top app bar with "Pengaturan" (or a large title).
2. Account row at the top (avatar, name, email/phone, chevron).
3. Grouped sections with headers (title-small): rows with a leading icon only if every row in the
   app's settings has one; trailing switch, value or chevron.
4. Destructive actions (sign out, delete account) last, separated, in the error role as text.
5. App version in body-small at the very end.

**Budget:** ≤7 rows per group; one icon family; switches act immediately (no save button).

**Fails:** coloured circles behind every icon; a save button for switches; sign out at the top;
nested settings deeper than two levels.

**States:** a switch that needs the network (pending, then confirmed or reverted with a message),
permission denied by the OS (explain and link to system settings).
