# Onboarding

**Job:** get a new person to the first moment of value, fast.

**Anatomy**
1. One welcome screen: what the app does in one sentence (headline-large), one visual, one primary
   action ("Mulai"), a secondary "Sudah punya akun? Masuk".
2. At most 3 setup steps that change the experience (goal, preference), each one question, with a
   progress indicator and a skip where possible.
3. Permissions asked in context, when the feature needs them, with a one-line reason before the
   system prompt.
4. Land on a home that is already useful (seeded with their answers), not empty.

**Budget:** ≤3 steps before value; one question per screen; centred layout is fine here.

**Fails:** 4-5 swipeable feature slides; asking every permission on launch; a sign-up wall before
showing anything; a form with 8 fields as step one.

**States:** back to a previous step keeps answers; interrupted onboarding resumes; permission
denied continues without the feature.
