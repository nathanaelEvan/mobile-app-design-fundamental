# Third-party notices

This skill is original work. It adapts ideas, and in a few places short passages, from the projects
below. Where it adapts, it changes them to fit modern-web-guidance and Material conventions.

## fluid-interactions

`references/motion.md` adapts spring and gesture principles from it. These are response and
damping springs, release projection, interruptibility, rubber banding, direction lock, morph
legibility and the reduced-motion rules. The custom swipe, sheet, scroller and morph
implementations are replaced by native patterns. iOS values are replaced by Material ones.
